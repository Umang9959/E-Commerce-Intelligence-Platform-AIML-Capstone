Q: How do I return a product?
A: This classroom demo uses a static FAQ. In a real store, customers should check the official return policy and contact support with their order number.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: This demo cannot cancel live orders. It can only retrieve static support guidance. A real store would require the customer to use the official account or support channel.

Q: Where is my order?
A: This project does not connect to live delivery systems. For the capstone demo, explain that shipment tracking would require a real order-management integration.

Q: How do I find similar products?
A: Use the recommendation screen. It finds products commonly purchased together in historical transactions.

Q: How does product search work?
A: The search screen compares the user query with product descriptions using TF-IDF and cosine similarity.

Q: Is the churn prediction final business advice?
A: No. It is a case-study model that estimates risk from historical purchase behavior and should be validated before any real business use.
